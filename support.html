What RepayPath does

RepayPath estimates how a student loan behaves under the repayment rules published by GOV.UK: how much is deducted from your payslip each month, when — or whether — the loan is repaid in full, how much interest builds up, and what happens if you overpay. Everything is calculated on your iPhone. There is no account and no connection to any server.

Frequently asked questions

Is this official? Is it connected to the Student Loans Company?

No. RepayPath is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Student Loans Company (SLC), HMRC, or the UK government. All rules used are publicly available on GOV.UK, and the app shows the date they were last checked.

Where do the numbers come from?

Income thresholds, repayment percentages and write-off periods come from GOV.UK — Repaying your student loan, last verified on 2 November 2026. The interest rate is set by the government and changes monthly, so the app lets you enter your own rate instead of showing a figure that may be out of date.

Why does the app say my loan will never be repaid?

Repayment is a percentage of income above a threshold — the balance itself does not affect how much you pay. If your income stays close to the threshold, repayments can be smaller than the interest added, so the balance never reaches zero and the loan is written off after a fixed number of years. That is a real feature of the system, not a bug in the app.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. RepayPath works entirely offline.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Inputs and saved plans are stored locally. Nothing is uploaded, and deleting the app deletes the data.

Can this app tell me what to do with my money?

No. It performs arithmetic only and does not provide financial, debt, or investment advice. For decisions about your loan, speak to the Student Loans Company or an authorised adviser.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app (long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete App). There is no copy on any server.
