RepayPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

RepayPath is an offline student loan repayment calculator. Your privacy is trivially protected: the app does not collect anything.

Data Collection

RepayPath does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, account credentials, or any identifier.

No Accounts, No Network, No Tracking

The app requires no account and makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party libraries that could observe your activity.

What You Enter Stays on the Device

The figures you type (loan balance, salary, interest rate) and any plans you save are stored in the app's own local storage on your iPhone. They are not backed up to any server we operate — we operate none. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

RepayPath requests no system permissions: no photo library, no location, no contacts, no notifications, no tracking authorisation.

Children

The app is a calculator and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.
